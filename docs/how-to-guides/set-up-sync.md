# Set Up Sync

By default, Joplin stores your notes only on the device you're using. Setting up sync lets you back up your notes to a cloud service and access the same notes from multiple devices — for example, your laptop and your phone.

This guide covers how to access sync settings, the sync options available, and how to connect one.

## Step 1: Open sync settings

There are two ways to reach the synchronisation settings:

1. **Via the menu** — go to **File → Synchronisation**
2. **Via the sidebar** — click the **Synchronisation** button in the left navigation panel
![Sync option using left navigation window](/img/Synchorinzation.png)

Both paths open the same window, where you can choose and configure a sync target (the service Joplin will sync your notes with).

## Step 2: Choose a sync option

Joplin offers several sync targets. Here are the three most commonly available:
![A screenshot that display list of sync options available](/img/Sync-Options.png)

### Joplin Cloud

Joplin's own official sync service, built specifically for Joplin.
Besides basic note syncing, it includes two extra features not available with the other options:

- **Publishing notes** — share individual notes as public web pages
- **Collaboration** — work on shared notebooks with other people

Using Joplin Cloud requires a Joplin Cloud account. If you don't already have one, you'll need to sign up for a subscription plan based on your needs (for example, storage size or number of devices).

### Dropbox

Syncs your notes using your existing Dropbox account and storage. This is a good option if you already use Dropbox for other files and want your notes to live alongside them. It does not include publishing or collaboration features — it's sync only.

### OneDrive

Syncs your notes using your existing Microsoft OneDrive account, similarly to Dropbox. Like Dropbox, it provides sync only, without publishing or collaboration features.

:::note
This list reflects the sync options accessible without a paid plan. Additional or more advanced sync targets may exist behind Joplin Cloud's subscription tiers.
:::

## Step 3: Connect your chosen sync target

**If you choose Joplin Cloud:**
1. Enter your email and password if you already have an account
![Login page](/img/Login-page.png)
2. If you don't have an account yet, choose a subscription plan and sign up
![Subscription Plans](/img/Plans.png)
3. Once logged in, Joplin will use your Joplin Cloud account as the sync destination

**If you choose Dropbox or OneDrive:**
1. Select the service from the sync target options
2. You'll be prompted to log in to your existing account for that service
3. Approve the connection when asked

## Step 4: Run your first sync

Once a sync target is connected, click **Synchronise**. Joplin will upload your existing notes to the connected service. Depending on how many notes you have, this may take a few seconds to a few minutes.

## Why this matters

Setting up sync means your notes aren't tied to a single device — if your computer is lost, damaged, or replaced, your notes remain safe and accessible from wherever you're synced.
